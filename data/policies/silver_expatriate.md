# Silver Expatriate Health Policy

Policy code: SI-EXP-2026
Product line: Meridian Global Health -- Silver tier, Expatriate plan

## Cover Levels

Inpatient (hospital) treatment is covered at 85% of
eligible costs. Outpatient treatment is covered up to USD 2,000 per policy year. The annual claim limit for this policy is
USD 150,000. Dental cover: basic dental (check-ups, fillings) covered.

## Exclusions

The following are excluded from all claims under this policy:

- cosmetic or elective surgery not required for a diagnosed medical condition
- self-inflicted injury
- injury or illness arising from war, invasion, or civil unrest
- experimental or unlicensed treatment
- drugs or treatment not prescribed by a registered medical practitioner
- injury sustained while under the influence of alcohol or non-prescribed drugs

Pre-existing medical conditions are excluded for the first 12 months unless declared and accepted at inception.

## Waiting Periods

Maternity cover is subject to a 18-month waiting
period from the policy start date. General practitioner (GP) consultations are subject to a 14-day waiting period from the policy start date. Specialist consultations are subject to a 60-day waiting period from the policy start date.

## Claims Procedure

Claims must be submitted within 120 days of treatment.
Claims method: reimbursement only: submit itemised receipts to the claims team.

## Geographic Scope

Cover applies worldwide, including medical evacuation and repatriation to the policyholder's home country when locally unavailable treatment is required.

## Additional Terms

Security evacuation (civil unrest, natural disaster) is covered up to USD 100,000 per policy year in addition to medical evacuation.
