# Bronze Student Health Policy

Policy code: BR-STU-2026
Product line: Meridian Global Health -- Bronze tier, Student plan

## Cover Levels

Inpatient (hospital) treatment is covered at 70% of
eligible costs. Outpatient treatment is not covered. The annual claim limit for this policy is
USD 50,000. Dental cover: not covered.

## Exclusions

The following are excluded from all claims under this policy:

- cosmetic or elective surgery not required for a diagnosed medical condition
- self-inflicted injury
- injury or illness arising from war, invasion, or civil unrest
- experimental or unlicensed treatment
- drugs or treatment not prescribed by a registered medical practitioner
- injury sustained while under the influence of alcohol or non-prescribed drugs

Pre-existing medical conditions are excluded permanently unless declared and accepted at inception.

## Waiting Periods

Maternity cover is subject to a 24-month waiting
period from the policy start date. General practitioner (GP) consultations are subject to a 30-day waiting period from the policy start date. Specialist consultations are subject to a 90-day waiting period from the policy start date.

## Claims Procedure

Claims must be submitted within 90 days of treatment.
Claims method: reimbursement only: submit itemised receipts to the claims team.

## Geographic Scope

Cover applies only within the policyholder's declared country of study, for the duration of the academic year stated on the policy schedule.

## Additional Terms

Cover ends automatically on course completion or after 12 months, whichever is sooner, unless renewed before expiry. Mental health support is included only on Gold and Platinum student policies.
