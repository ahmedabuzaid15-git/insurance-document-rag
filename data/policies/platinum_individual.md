# Platinum Individual Health Policy

Policy code: PL-IND-2026
Product line: Meridian Global Health -- Platinum tier, Individual plan

## Cover Levels

Inpatient (hospital) treatment is covered at 100% of
eligible costs. Outpatient treatment is covered with no annual cap. The annual claim limit for this policy is
unlimited. Dental cover: comprehensive dental and optical cover included.

## Exclusions

The following are excluded from all claims under this policy:

- cosmetic or elective surgery not required for a diagnosed medical condition
- self-inflicted injury
- injury or illness arising from war, invasion, or civil unrest
- experimental or unlicensed treatment
- drugs or treatment not prescribed by a registered medical practitioner
- injury sustained while under the influence of alcohol or non-prescribed drugs

Pre-existing medical conditions are covered after 12 months of continuous cover on this policy.

## Waiting Periods

Maternity cover is subject to a 9-month waiting
period from the policy start date. General practitioner (GP) consultations are covered from the policy start date with no waiting period. Specialist consultations are covered from the policy start date with no waiting period.

## Claims Procedure

Claims must be submitted within 180 days of treatment.
Claims method: direct billing at network providers, or reimbursement with receipts.

## Geographic Scope

Cover applies in the policyholder's country of residence, with emergency treatment covered worldwide for the first 30 days of any trip.

No additional clauses apply beyond the standard tier terms below.
